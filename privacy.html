Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MatchZone ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our football scores and information application.

1. Information and App Permissions

Depending on the features you use, MatchZone may process app preferences, favorite teams and leagues, profile images you choose, notification settings, and technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the app.

If you choose to take a profile picture, the app may request camera access. If you choose an existing picture, it may request access to your photos or media. You can manage app permissions through your device settings.

We will update this policy to accurately identify any additional personal or sensitive information collected by the released app.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide football scores, fixtures, standings, team information, save preferences, support profile features, deliver notifications you enable, maintain security, and troubleshoot app problems.

3. Storage and Third-Party Services

MatchZone may store preferences on your device and may use external services to provide football data or other app functionality. The information those services process depends on the integrations enabled in the released app.

Before this policy is finalized, the developer must identify the third-party services actually used and link to their relevant privacy policies. Any applicable Firebase services, analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services must be disclosed accurately.

4. Sharing of Information

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to deliver app functionality, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations. The final policy must identify the applicable categories of information and recipients based on the app's actual data practices.

We do not claim that information is never shared or collected by third-party services without first verifying those services and their configurations.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you remove it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Information processed by external services may be subject to their retention and deletion practices.

Any account or cloud-stored information must have an appropriate deletion process described here if those features are provided.

6. Children's Privacy

MatchZone's intended audience and any data practices involving children must be assessed in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play policies. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable requirements.

7. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information handled by MatchZone. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The current version will be published at this webpage.
