Delete a MatchZone Account

MatchZone users can permanently delete an account and associated app data.

Inside the app

  1. Open MatchZone and go to Profile.
  2. Open Account & Privacy.
  3. Choose Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your password when requested and approve permanent deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email from the address connected to your MatchZone account. Use the subject "MatchZone account deletion". Requests are verified before deletion.

Email deletion request

If Gmail does not open, email us directly at brosetechytb@gmail.com.

Data deleted

The Firebase account, fan-room messages, votes, reports, blocks, signed-in feedback and local profile picture are deleted. Device-only preferences and cached public football information can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling MatchZone.
