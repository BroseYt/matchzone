MatchZone Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MatchZone accesses, uses, stores and deletes information.

Information handled

MatchZone may process an email address, Firebase user ID, fan-room messages, poll votes, reports, blocks, feedback, notification preferences and an Expo push token when notifications are enabled. A profile picture selected in the app remains on the user's device and is not uploaded by MatchZone.

Purposes

Information is used to authenticate users, provide account features, operate and moderate fan rooms, save feedback, deliver requested notifications, prevent abuse and maintain security.

Service providers

MatchZone uses Google Firebase for authentication and database features, Expo for app build and notification delivery, football-data.org, API-Football and ESPN for football information, and BBC Sport and Sky Sports feeds for news links and related images. Official media and broadcaster services process information under their own privacy terms when users open their links.

Permissions

Camera access is requested only when a user chooses to take a profile picture. The system photo picker is used to select an existing picture. Notification permission is requested only when alerts are enabled.

Retention and deletion

Account information is retained while an account is active. Users can delete their account and associated MatchZone data from Profile > Delete Account or use the public deletion process. Information may be retained only when required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent abuse.

Children

MatchZone is a general-audience sports app and is not directed to children under 13. Fan rooms require a signed-in account and acceptance of community rules.
