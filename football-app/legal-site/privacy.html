MatchZone Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MatchZone handles information when you use our football scores, news, notifications, and fan community features.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, MatchZone may handle the following information:

  • Account information: Your email address, Firebase user identifier, and information needed to authenticate your account.
  • Fan community information: Messages you post in fan rooms, poll votes, reports you submit, blocking information, and associated timestamps.
  • Feedback: Feedback you submit through the app, together with the information associated with that submission.
  • Notification information: Your notification preferences and, when push alerts are registered, your Expo push token and related match-alert subscriptions.
  • Profile pictures: Pictures you choose or take for your profile. The current implementation stores these locally on your device rather than uploading them to MatchZone's servers.
  • Football and news requests: MatchZone requests football scores, fixtures, statistics, player information, and news-feed content from external providers to deliver app features.

You can browse core football information without creating an account. Account-based features, such as participating in fan rooms, require sign-in.

2. How We Use Information

Information is handled as needed to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Provide fan rooms, community polls, and related features.
  • Receive and manage reports and user-blocking requests.
  • Store and respond to user feedback.
  • Register and deliver requested match notifications.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the service against misuse.
  • Provide football data, statistics, and news-related content.

3. Services and Third Parties

MatchZone relies on third-party services to operate certain features:

  • Google Firebase: Used for account authentication and database functionality, including account-related and fan community information.
  • Expo: Used for app infrastructure and push notification delivery.
  • Cloudflare: Used to run the server-side service that retrieves football data and news feeds and manages match-alert registrations and related cached information.
  • football-data.org, API-Football, and ESPN: Used as sources of football fixtures, scores, statistics, and related football information.
  • BBC Sport and Sky Sports: Their feeds may be used to display news links and retrieve related article images.

These providers may process information necessary to deliver their services. Their handling of information is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

If you open an external news article or other third-party link, you leave MatchZone and become subject to the destination service's privacy practices.

4. Device Permissions and Local Storage

  • Camera: If you choose to take a profile picture, MatchZone may request camera access for that purpose.
  • Photos: You can choose an existing picture for your profile using the available photo-selection interface.
  • Notifications: If you enable push alerts, MatchZone may request permission to send notifications.
  • Local storage: MatchZone stores certain preferences and profile-picture files on your device. You can remove your local profile picture through the app's available controls.

Profile pictures are stored locally in the current implementation and are not uploaded to MatchZone's servers. You can manage device permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Account and community information may be stored using Firebase. Notification registrations and related match-alert information may be stored by MatchZone's server infrastructure. Some football data is cached to support performance and reduce repeated requests.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant features, maintain service security, handle reports, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention periods can differ depending on the type of information and the service involved.

Cached football information is operational data and may remain after a user deletes their account. Deleting an account does not necessarily remove information independently retained by third-party providers under their own policies.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can initiate account deletion through the app's Profile > Delete Account option, where available, or follow the instructions on our account-deletion page.

The account-deletion process removes the Firebase account and associated MatchZone records covered by the deletion process. Certain local files and preferences are also removed by the app. If deletion fails or the app cannot complete the process, contact us using the email address below for assistance.

Some operational records, cached football information, or information that must be retained for legitimate legal or security reasons may not be removed immediately. Information retained for these reasons will be handled according to the applicable retention requirements.

7. Your Choices

Depending on the features you use, you can:

  • Browse core football information without an account.
  • Manage notification permissions and preferences.
  • Remove your locally stored profile picture.
  • Report or block users in fan rooms.
  • Request account deletion using the available process.
  • Contact us with questions about your information.

8. Security

We use the security features provided by our infrastructure and take reasonable steps to protect information handled by MatchZone. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

MatchZone is a general-audience sports application and is not directed to children under 13. Fan-room participation requires a signed-in account and acceptance of the community rules.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MatchZone's features, services, or legal obligations change. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.
